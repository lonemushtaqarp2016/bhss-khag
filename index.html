About Our Institution

Pioneering Secondary & Higher Education in Khag Valley

Government Boys Higher Secondary School Khag stands as a pillar of learning in District Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir. Situated at the foothills of the scenic Khag mountains, our institution provides a vibrant academic environment for secondary (9th & 10th) and higher secondary (11th & 12th) students.
